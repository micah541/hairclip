\subsection{Definition}
Throughout, $\tau_1$ is the hand-off time and $P(\tau)=(t,\lambda,\xi,\alpha)(\tau)$ is the modulation path of Proposition~(P2$'$) of \fn{U1_regularity_v44.tex}, defined on its maximal interval $[\tau_1,\tau_{\rm mod})$ with values in the open set $\mathcal O$ (built from $\mathrm{GS}^\circ$ and the relaxed validity box).
\begin{definition}[the bootstrap set $\mathrm{BS}$]\label{BS:def}
For $\tau'\in[\tau_1,\tau_{\rm mod})$, $\mathrm{BS}(\tau')$ means that the following hold for every $\tau\in[\tau_1,\tau']$.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=2pt]

\item[B1] (verifier \fn{fin13v4c}) (a) $|\alpha|$ below the $\alpha$-barrier of the march and $\le a^*\lambda$ in the tail; (b) $\Lambda\le L(\tau)+2C'$ on the march and $\Lambda\le\ell(\tau)+2C'$ in the tail, $2C'\le\TwoCprimeV$ (flux lemma, Proposition~\ref{F:prop:abs}); (c) the \emph{box part}: the hypotheses (B-a)--(B-f), (E0), (E2) of the $E_I$ lemma (\fn{ei_lemma_v4c_v44.tex} \S\ref{EI:sec:hyp}) and the D-2a box of the strip cubic term H8: $|\alpha|\le2.5\cdot10^{-4}$; $|w|,|w'|\le10^{-3}$ on $[10.25,16.25]$; $|w'|\le0.02$ on $[16.25,60]$; $|w|\le0.02$ on $[0,34.25]$ and $|w|\le\frac12$ on $[34.25,60]$; $|w|,|w'|\le10^{-4}$ on $[0,8]$; $|w|,|w'|\le0.01$ and $|\alpha|\le10^{-3}$ on $[16.25,31.25]$; the modulation validity box $|\alpha|\le5\cdot10^{-4}$, $A,W_1\le10^{-3}$, $|w|,|w'|,|w''|\le0.02$ on $\sigma\in[29,32]$.

\end{definition}
\noindent\emph{Members without a gap (text v40, review fix18 F-1).} B1(a) and B1(b) are members (every step that consumes them assumes them on $[\tau_1,\tau']$), but the implication step returns them in the \emph{same} form: $Y=\Lambda-Z\le L$ gives $\Lambda\le L+2C'$ again, because $Z$ can reach $2C'$; the $\alpha$-barrier of the march is its own conclusion, and in the tail the bound $a^*\lambda$ has no cushion. Every other member has a uniform gap.

\noindent\emph{What is not a member.} (H1)--(H6), (H2f), GS at $\tau_1$ are hand-off data; the constants $\Eh,\Bh$ are certified numbers; the flux inputs $\fn{FLUX0},\fn{FLUX2}$ are typed constants of the verifier justified by (I3$'$) (a conclusion of the flux lemma under BF and B2$'$). \subsection{Closure}\label{BS:sec:close}
\begin{proposition}[closure of $\mathrm{BS}$]\label{BS:prop:close}
Assume the hand-off (H1)--(H6), (H2f) at $\tau_1$ (Theorem~\ref{M:thm:W}), GS at $\tau_1$ (GS-1), and the closure conditions (K1)--(K6) of \fn{lemmaU_v44.tex} Proposition ``closure'' together with the conditions (C0)--(C7) of Proposition~\ref{F:prop:abs}. Then $\mathrm{BS}(\tau')$ holds for every $\tau'<\tau_{\rm mod}$. If $\tau_{\rm mod}<\infty$ then $t(\tau)\uparrow t_1+T_{\max}$ as $\tau\uparrow\tau_{\rm mod}$; and $\tau_{\rm mod}=\tau_{\max}=\infty$ by Proposition~\ref{C:prop:global}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}\tHd\ Let $\mathcal T=\{\tau'\in[\tau_1,\tau_{\rm mod}):\mathrm{BS}(\tau')\}$ and $\tau^*=\sup\mathcal T$.

\emph{Nonempty.} At $\tau_1$: GS by GS-1 ($\GStauKuV<\frac12$, $\GStauUpV<1$, \fn{fix14_interface/GS_tau1.json}); B1(a,b) by (H1) ($|\alpha(\tau_1)|\le\alpha_1$, $\sqrt{\Lambda(\tau_1)}\le5\cdot10^{-8}$, below the first cell) and B1(c) by the first cell's box assertion; BF: (F1) holds for windows of length $0$ trivially and $|(a,b)(\tau_1)|\le\bar a$ by the modulation lemma at the hand-off state (the verifier's first-cell value); B2$'$, B3U, B4, G at $\tau_1$ by (K6) and (H2)--(H5) as in \fn{lemmaU_v44.tex} (``Nonempty''). So $\tau_1\in\mathcal T$.

\emph{Implication step.} Let $\tau'\in\mathcal T$. Follow the order table. U-1 gives the energy identities at every $\tau\le\tau'$. The verifier's cells, with (I3$'$) from the flux lemma (whose inputs BF, B2$'$, B1(c) hold), give B1(a,b) again (not strictly: no gap) in the form $Y\le L$, $Y\le\ell$ (Proposition~\ref{F:prop:abs}, conditions (C0)--(C7)), and the asserted box ratios of B1(c), all $\le\BdV<1$, at every state with $\Lambda\le\mathrm{Ly}^{hi}$, which B1(b) and (C2) cover. (A) gives B2$'$ strictly and the exports, hence (F1) without the factor 2; D1's \fn{mod_sups} give (F2) with factor $\times4.67$. U-crude, U-fine, D5a, D5b give B3U, B4 strictly; Lemma~\ref{C:lem:G} gives G strictly; Lemma GS-2 gives GS strictly. Each improved constant is a fixed number (independent of $\tau'$), so the gaps are uniform, for every member except B1(a,b).

\emph{Closed.} Suppose $\tau^*<\tau_{\rm mod}$. First GS and G at $\tau^*$ (text v41, delta check \fn{fix20_delta_check/DELTA_CHECK_v40.md} W-1: they use only the members on $[\tau_1,\tau^*)$, the continuity of $P$ and the smoothness of the flow at $t(\tau^*)$, not U-1 at $\tau^*$). G: Lemma~\ref{C:lem:G}. GS: Lemma GS-4 of \fn{GS_lemma_v44.tex} (limits of graph functions with the strict bounds of GS-2; the solution is smooth at $t(\tau^*)<t_1+T_{\max}$ because $\tau^*<\tau_{\rm mod}$). So GS and G hold on $[\tau_1,\tau^*]$, and Theorem U-1 of \fn{U1_regularity_v44.tex} applies on $[\tau_1,\tau^*]$. Each other member except (F1) and B3U(a) is an inequality $Q(\tau)\le c$ for a function $Q$ that is defined and continuous wherever GS and G hold, in particular on $[\tau_1,\tau^*]$ (for B1(a,b) the right side is the continuous barrier function of $\tau$) (Theorem U-1: $P$, $\Lambda$, the energies and the sups of $w,w'$ on compact $x$-sets are continuous in $\tau$); these pass to $\tau^*$. (F1): the window integrals are continuous in the window end (U-1(d)), so the bound passes to windows ending at $\tau^*$. B3U(a): joint continuity of $(\tau,\rho)\mapsto\E^\rho_j(\tau)$ (\fn{lemmaU_v44.tex}, ``Closed''). Hence $\tau^*\in\mathcal T$.

\emph{Open} (text v40: rewritten for review fix18 F-1). At $\tau^*$ all members hold, and all except B1(a,b) hold with the uniform gaps of the implication step. $P(\tau^*)\in\mathfrak B_{\rm mod}\subset\mathcal O$ and $P$ is continuous on $[\tau_1,\tau_{\rm mod})$ ((P2$'$)(b)); take $\delta>0$ with $\tau^*+\delta<\tau_{\rm mod}$, to be decreased below.

\emph{Step 1: the members with a gap} (in this order: GS and G, then U-1, then the other members; text v41, delta check \fn{fix20_delta_check/DELTA_CHECK_v40.md} W-1). GS: Lemma GS-3 (openness with the endpoint $x=\pm60$) gives GS on $[\tau^*,\tau^*+\delta]$. G: Lemma~\ref{C:lem:G} (``Open''). Both need only the continuity of $P$ ((P2$'$)(b)) and the smooth flow. Theorem U-1 now applies on $[\tau_1,\tau^*+\delta]$: it needs only GS, G and $\tau^*+\delta<\tau_{\rm mod}$; it gives the continuity used in the rest of Step~1. The scalar members other than B1(a,b) (the box part B1(c), worst ratio $\BdV<1$; (F2); B2$'$; the scalar parts of B3U and B4): continuity and the gaps. (F1): the extra piece of a window beyond $\tau^*$ tends to $0$ uniformly in the window start (local integrability, U-1(d); \fn{flux2_lemma_v44.tex} Remark~\ref{F:rem:boot}), so the gap of $\times2$ persists for $\delta$ small. B3U(a): as in \fn{lemmaU_v44.tex} (``Open''). No step of Step~1 uses B1(a,b) beyond $\tau^*$. So for $\delta$ small every member except B1(a,b) holds on $[\tau_1,\tau^*+\delta]$.

\emph{Step 2: B1(a,b) by first touch.} Theorem U-1 applies on $[\tau_1,\tau^*+\delta]$ (Step~1). Apply Proposition~\ref{F:prop:abs} on $[\tau_1,\tau^*+\delta]$. Its proof is a first-touch argument for the pair $(|\alpha|,Y)$: in $Y$ on every march step and in the tail (direct), and for $\alpha$: in the tail the comparison of \fn{step3nl_v4c/verifier/TAIL_LEMMA.tex}, section ``Tail contraction (first touch)'' (cooperative: $\beta$ is nondecreasing in $\Lambda$); on a march step the $\alpha$-barrier is the per-cell fixed point of \fn{closure.py}:113--120, 134, and it needs only $\Lambda\le\mathrm{Ly}^{hi}_k$, which holds up to and just beyond a first touch of $Y=L$ by (C2) (Proposition~\ref{F:prop:abs}, ``the $\alpha$-barrier is unchanged''; text v41, delta check \fn{fix20_delta_check/DELTA_CHECK_v40.md} W-3). Its inputs are: the energy identities and inequalities of U-1(b), (c); inequality (F:eq:star) of the flux lemma, which needs BF, B2$'$ and B1(c); the cell certificates, valid for every state of a cell under the box part B1(c); and the certified conditions (C0)--(C7). All of them hold on $[\tau_1,\tau^*+\delta]$ by Step~1. B1(a,b) themselves are not assumed beyond $\tau^*$: the first touch starts at $\tau_1$, where (H1) puts the state below the first cell. Hence B1(a,b) hold on $[\tau_1,\tau^*+\delta]$.

So $\tau^*+\delta\in\mathcal T$, contradicting the definition of $\tau^*$. So $\tau^*=\tau_{\rm mod}$, i.e.\ $\mathrm{BS}(\tau')$ for all $\tau'<\tau_{\rm mod}$.

\emph{End of the interval.} Since $P(\tau)\in\mathfrak B_{\rm mod}$ for all $\tau<\tau_{\rm mod}$, Proposition~(P2$'$)(c) gives $t(\tau)\uparrow t_1+T_{\max}$ if $\tau_{\rm mod}<\infty$. Proposition~\ref{C:prop:global} then shows that $\int\kappa^2ds$ stays bounded, which contradicts Lemma ``blow-up criterion'' of \fn{U1_regularity_v44.tex}; so $\tau_{\rm mod}=\infty$, and $\tau_{\max}=\tau_{\rm mod}$.
\end{proof}
