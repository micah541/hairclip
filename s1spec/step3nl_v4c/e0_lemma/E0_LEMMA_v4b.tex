\begin{lemma}[E0 couplings]\label{lem}
Assume $|\alpha|\le\alpha_E:=2.5\cdot10^{-4}$, $|w|\le0.02$ on $[0,34.25]$, and the hypotheses of the modulation lemma
($A,W_1\le10^{-3}$, $|\alpha|\le5\cdot10^{-4}$, strip $S_0,S_1,S_2\le0.02$); all are asserted along the march (\texttt{closure.box\_assert}).
Let $\mathcal D_{\rm core}$ be the $\alpha$-linear part of the nonlinear drift paired on $\sigma\le29$. Then
\[
\mathcal M+\ip{\chi\Phi_F}{\wt}+\mathcal D_{\rm core}\le \Pi_3A^2+M_\alpha|\alpha|A^2+f\,F_n(\alpha)A+n_{123}|\beta_S|A+7.52\,q_\beta QA+\sum_{i=3}^{7}\mathcal H_i,
\]
with the CODED constants $\Pi_3=0.01607$, $M_\alpha=25$, $f=5.72$, $n_{123}=6.588186$ (replacing the typed 7.52 in the $\beta_S$ term),
$q_\beta=5.5264$, $F_n\ge\|\chi\Phi_F\|$, and the coded state terms $\mathcal H_3,\dots,\mathcal H_7$ of (v) below.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
(i) \emph{Projection} [cert]. $\ip{g_0}{\wt}=\ip{\Pi g_0}{\wt}$ and $\|\Pi g_0\|\le n_{123}=6.588186$ (Gram \texttt{gram7\_g3.json} [read], lemma $G_{33}\ge0.031107$);
$\|g_0\|\le n_3=7.69698$. Every generic coupling $|\beta_\bullet|\,|\ip{g_0}{\wt}|$ costs $n_{123}|\beta_\bullet|A$; the typed 7.52 ($q_\beta$ term) is valid because $7.52\ge n_{123}$.

(ii) \emph{Rank-one symmetrisation} [sym I1 + cert]. $\sup_{\|w\|=1}|\ip{w}{u}\ip{w}{v}|=\tfrac12(\|u\|\|v\|+|\ip uv|)$. With $\|u_3\|\le\varepsilon_1=6.6452\cdot10^{-5}$
and $|\ip{u_3}{g_0}|\le X=1.0238\cdot10^{-5}$ ($u_3$ lives on the strip and the tip bumps; $\sup_{\rm strip}|\hat\psi_3|\le719.62$) [cert],
$|\beta_3\ip{g_0}{\wt}|\le\tfrac12H_{33}(\varepsilon_1n_3+X)A^2$, $H_{33}\le32.1486$. With $|\beta_{12}|\le3.0345\cdot10^{-4}A$ and the lemma's $d_{\rm lead}$:
$\Pi_3^{\rm true}=n_{123}(3.0345\cdot10^{-4}+d_{\rm lead})+\tfrac12H_{33}(\varepsilon_1n_3+X)=0.0103854$ [cert].

(iii) \emph{Forcing} [cert]. $\ip{\chi\Phi_F}{\wt}-\beta_F\ip{g_0}{\wt}\le(1+n_{123}\beta_F^{\rm coef})F_nA=5.32533\,F_nA$; the oblique-projection identity
$\|I-h\otimes g_0\|=\|h\|\|g_0\|$ for $\ip{g_0}{h}=1$ [sym I2] gives the sharper 5.0196 (not used). All other $F_n$-proportional pieces of $\mathcal M$
(the $(a,b)$ forcing in $Q_1$ and in the loop, $\beta_F$ in the $\hat h_2$ and $E_{33}$ terms, the $H_{31},H_{32}$ part, the $\alpha^2$ remainders) add $0.01862$ at $\alpha_E$:
total $5.34395\le5.72$ (margin $\times1.070$).

(iv) \emph{The $\alpha A^2$ budget} [cert given the reads]. $Q_{\rm full}$ (first-order $Q_1$ plus the $\beta$-loop, \texttt{bloop.json} [read]) $\le6.19418|\alpha|A^2$ on
$\{f_1,f_2,f_3\}^\perp$. Not in $Q_{\rm full}$, per $|\alpha|A^2$ at $\alpha_E$: $n_{123}m_{\rm new}=29.5274$ ($\beta_{\rm Lin}$, $m_{\rm new}=4.48188$ [read]),
loop remainders $0.0630$, $(a,b)$ $\alpha$-corrections in $Q_1$ $1.2492$ and in the loop $0.8765$, $\hat h_2$ term $0.0640$, $E_{33}$ feedback $0.0174$, and the drift
$\mathcal D_{\rm core}\le C_{\rm dr}|\alpha|A^2$, $C_{\rm dr}=0.549308$ [cert; identity [sym]: $\int\rho Xw\,D_{\rm lin}(w)=\int\rho c_{\rm dr}w^2$,
$c_{\rm dr}=\tfrac18X[q'G-\phi'qG+q(mu_0-ku_0')(1-ku_0)^{-2}]+\tfrac18X'qG$, $G=ku_0/(1-ku_0)$, boundary term 0 since $q(0)=0$]. Total need $N(\alpha_E)=38.5411$.
As $N(\alpha)=c_0+c_1|\alpha|$ with $c_1\ge0$ and $N>25$, $(N(\alpha)-25)|\alpha|\le(N(\alpha_E)-25)\alpha_E=3.385\cdot10^{-3}$, and
$\Pi_3^{\rm true}+3.385\cdot10^{-3}=0.01377\le0.01607=\Pi_3$ (slack $5.685\cdot10^{-3}$, margin $\times1.679$; $\times2.786$ at $|\alpha|\le1.6\cdot10^{-4}$).

(v) \emph{Higher-order modulation pieces, coded} [cert constants; hand bounds, CS + $|(a,b)|\le AB$, $|\beta-\beta_{\rm lead}|\le\beta_1|\alpha|A+q_\beta Q+\beta_S+\beta_F^{\rm coef}F_n$].
With $AB=AB_x+AB_c$ and $BT_x$ the state bounds of the modulation lemma, $AB_s,BT_s$ its strip forms, $\|\chi w'\|\le W_1+\|\chi'w\|$, $\|\chi'w\|\le\tfrac{35}{48}I_R^{1/2}\sup_{\rm strip}|w|$:
$\mathcal H_3=AB[(1+C_{A0}+C_{B0})A^2+(C_{A1}+C_{B1})A(W_1+\|\chi'w\|)]$ ($w$-parts of $A_1(u)-A_1(u_0)$, $B_1(u)-B_1(u_0)$ [sym I4]);
$\mathcal H_4=|\alpha|(\|(g_A,g_B)\|+L_C)(q_{ab}Q+AB_s)A$ ($(a,b)$ beyond its lead in $Q_1$ and in the loop);
$\mathcal H_5=AB_x\alpha^2(R_{A2}+R_{B2})A$ ($\alpha^2$ remainders of $A_1(u_0),B_1(u_0)$ [sym I6]);
$\mathcal H_6=|\alpha|H_{OB}(\beta_1|\alpha|A+q_\beta Q+BT_s)A$ ($\beta$ beyond its lead in the $\hat h_2$ and $E_{33}$ terms);
$\mathcal H_7=|\alpha|(H_{12}^{ab}AB_x+H_{12}^{\beta}BT_x)A$ ($H_{31},H_{32}$ part of $\beta_E$). Their $F_n$ parts are in (iii).
\end{proof}
